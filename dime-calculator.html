What the number doesn't tell you.

01

How long you need it

Most of this need shrinks over time as debts get paid and kids grow up. That affects whether term coverage, permanent coverage, or a mix fits the job.

02

Whether work coverage is enough

Coverage through work usually ends when the job does, and it often equals only one or two years of salary. Try turning it off above to see your gap without it.

03

What it costs

The price depends on your age, health, the term length, and the type of policy. Run the "What job does it need to do?" checklist before you buy any policy, from anyone.
